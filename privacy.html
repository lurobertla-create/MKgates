Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

1. Who we are

GateQuote ("we", "us", "our") operates a free quote-matching service connecting homeowners in Milton Keynes with independent garage door and electric gate specialists. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your postcode or address
  • Details of the service you're enquiring about
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Match your enquiry with suitable local specialists
  • Share your enquiry details with those specialists so they can contact you directly
  • Respond to questions you send us directly
  • Improve our service and understand how our website is used

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we only share your enquiry details with specialists relevant to the job you've described.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given when you submit a form on this site) and our legitimate interest in operating a quote-matching service effectively.

5. How long we keep your data

We retain enquiry data for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, typically no longer than 24 months, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@gatequote.co.uk.

7. Cookies

This site may use essential cookies required for basic functionality. It does not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes without your consent.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

9. Third-party specialists

Specialists matched through GateQuote are independent businesses. Once your enquiry is shared with a specialist, their own privacy practices will apply to any further data they collect from you directly.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at hello@gatequote.co.uk or 01908 000 000.

11. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
